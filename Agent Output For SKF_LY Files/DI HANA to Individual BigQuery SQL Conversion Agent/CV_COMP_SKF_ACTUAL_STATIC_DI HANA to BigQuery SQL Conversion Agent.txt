SOURCE_LOGIC:
This Calculation View is a CUBE with outputViewType="Aggregation", so the final output must GROUP BY all non-measure attributes and aggregate all measures using SUM as specified in the logicalModel. The data flow is as follows:
- WEEKLY_SNAPSHOT_DS05 filters the base actuals for a specific fiscal variant, week, version, audit trail, and account.
- CC_HIER projects nodes with specific parent node and validity.
- Join_3: INNER JOIN of WEEKLY_SNAPSHOT_DS05 and CC_HIER on _B631_S_COSTCNTR and MANDT.
- Projection_1: Projects all columns from Join_3 and adds CAL_WEEK as a constant ($$IP_WEEK_CY$$).
- Join_2: LEFT OUTER JOIN of Projection_1 with STORE_ATTR_ACTUAL on _B631_S_PROFTCTR and MANDT.
- Join_1: LEFT OUTER JOIN of Join_2 with RETAIL_CAL on CAL_WEEK = ZZWEEK.
- COMP_FLAG_ACT01: Projects COMP_FLAG data with filter on week and version.
- Join_4: LEFT OUTER JOIN of Join_1 with COMP_FLAG_ACT01 on _B631_S_PROFTCTR and MANDT.
- PROFIT_CENTER_TEXT: Projects profit center text.
- Join_5: LEFT OUTER JOIN of Join_4 with PROFIT_CENTER_TEXT on _B631_S_PROFTCTR and MANDT.
- FLAGS: Final projection from Join_5, adds CAL_WEEK_NUMBER and CAL_ZIO_SKFQ_QTY as calculated columns.
- Final output: All attributes and measures as specified in logicalModel, with SUM aggregation on measures and GROUP BY on all other attributes.
