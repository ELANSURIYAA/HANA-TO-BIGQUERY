- WEEKLY_SNAPSHOT_DS05 projects from CV_BASE_FIN_WEEKLY_ACTUAL_S4 with filter:

- Projection_1: Adds CAL_WEEK as '$$IP_WEEK_CY$$' (constant) to Join_6 output.

- Join_2: LEFT OUTER JOIN of Projection_1 and STORE_ATTR_ACTUAL on (_B631_S_PROFTCTR/PRCTR, MANDT), bringing in store attributes.

- Join_1: LEFT OUTER JOIN of Join_2 and RETAIL_CAL on (CAL_WEEK/ZZWEEK, MANDT/RCLNT), adds week start/end dates.
- WEEK_NUMBER: Projection of Join_1 (no additional logic).

    - ZWEEK = $$IP_WEEK_CY$$
    - COMP_VER = $$IP_VERSION_COMP_FLAG$$
- Join_3: LEFT OUTER JOIN of WEEK_NUMBER and COMP_FLAG_LY on (_B631_S_PROFTCTR/PRCTR, MANDT).
- PROFIT_CENTER_TEXT: Projects from CV_BASE_MD_CEPCT_S4, mapping LTEXT to PROFIT_CENTER_TEXT.

    AND j3.MANDT = pct.MANDT

  SELECT

    CASE
      WHEN LEFT(_BIC_ZWWPC_PA1, 2) = 'FS' THEN 'FS'
      WHEN LEFT(_BIC_ZWWPC_PA1, 2) = 'RX' THEN 'RX'
